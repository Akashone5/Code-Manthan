Disparate Impact Ratio (DIR):

Selection rate of unprivileged group divided by privileged group. A ratio ≥ 0.80 complies with the EEOC / Fair Housing four-fifths standard.

Equal Opportunity (TPR Parity):

True Positive Rate equality guarantees that creditworthy applicants from rural regions have an equal opportunity of being approved as urban peers.

Mission Bharat Impact:

Enabling calibration raises rural approval from 71.5% to 84.1%, elevating DIR from 0.83 to 0.97 and eliminating systematic geographic exclusion.
